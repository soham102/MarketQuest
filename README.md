# MarketQuest 📈

**Link:** [https://market-quest-beta.vercel.app/](https://market-quest-beta.vercel.app/)

MarketQuest is an interactive stock market learning platform designed to help beginners understand how the stock market works before investing their hard-earned money. It combines educational content with virtual trading simulations, allowing users to learn market concepts, experiment with trading decisions, and understand their potential impact without risking real money.

The platform bridges the gap between theoretical financial knowledge and practical market experience through hands-on learning, interactive lessons, and simulated investment scenarios.

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **Interactive Learning Modules:** Understand essential stock market concepts through beginner-friendly explanations.
- 📊 **10-Round Trading Simulation:** Experience simulated market scenarios and make decisions across multiple rounds.
- 💰 **Virtual Trading Portfolio:** Start with ₹1,00,000 in virtual money to practise trading without financial risk.
- 🧠 **Decision-Based Learning:** Explore how market events and trading choices can affect your virtual portfolio.
- 🎯 **Practical Market Experience:** Learn by making virtual investment decisions rather than relying only on theory.
- 📈 **Progressive Learning:** Build familiarity with stock market terminology, market behaviour, and portfolio decisions through practice.

## 🎯 Why MarketQuest?

For many first-time investors, entering the stock market can feel overwhelming. Questions like *Which stocks should I understand? How does the market work? What happens when prices rise or fall?* can make getting started difficult.

MarketQuest aims to make financial learning more accessible by providing a space where beginners can explore market concepts, test their understanding, and practise decision-making in a simulated environment.

Whether you're a student, a first-time earner, or someone curious about investing, MarketQuest offers a practical starting point for learning about the stock market before committing real money.

**The goal is simple: learn first, practise safely, and make more informed financial decisions.**

## 🛠️ Technology

MarketQuest is built as a web-based learning application and deployed on Vercel. Its development includes:

- **Next.js / React** — application interface and user experience
- **Supabase** — backend services and planned account-based progress persistence
- **Vercel** — deployment and hosting

*The exact implementation details may vary based on the current codebase.*

To make stock market education more interactive, approachable, and practical for beginners — helping people build financial literacy and confidence through learning and simulation before making real-world investment decisions.
